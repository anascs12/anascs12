### 🛰️ [AI Research Radar](https://github.com/anascs12/ai-research-radar)

**Problem:** thousands of AI papers appear every month. Which topics are rising, and can papers be found by meaning?

**Built:** embedded **12,060 arXiv papers** with sentence transformers, discovered 25 topics (k-means + class-based TF-IDF), measured trends, and built a semantic-search app.

**Finding:** LLM-agent papers grew **4.6×** in share (2.1% → 9.8%); about 23% of AI papers now mention agents.

### 🌍 [What Drives Life Expectancy?](https://github.com/anascs12/life-expectancy-insights)

**Problem:** how much of the gap in life expectancy between countries do income, schooling, health spending, water and fertility explain?

**Built:** a reproducible pipeline on Our World in Data's API (found by tracing its charts with a reverse-engineering tool), data-quality checks, and models validated on **countries never seen in training**.

**Finding:** six factors explain **80%** of the variation (R² 0.80, error 2.6 years); a simple linear model beat random forest and gradient boosting.
